   Fifteen control calls cost $0.7016625, but repeated exact-minimum topups were
   immediately consumed by formation fees. Sponsored applications further reduced
   parent runway. Skip unnecessary coordination when it would spend that reserve.
2. Drain pending handoff reviews and expose authoritative contract status in every
   relevant phase. Core waited rounds20-23 after another handoff consumed the bank
   review slot. Formation omitted contract state, prompting status questions and
   messages that woke already-accepted workers.
3. Keep established acceptance separate from a new bounded investigation. The
   final probe first hit an accepted-contract tool restriction; replacing the
   contract reopened core without preserving completed readiness as a distinct
   milestone. Any renewal must retain original obligations and strict checks.
4. Give an authorized final investigation enough bounded steps to compare its
   observation and close it. The bank requested a new boundary in round24, then
   selected before the returned probe could be compared locally in round25.
   Count useful evidence progress while preserving a strict time/spend bound.
5. Reduce avoidable receipt invalidation. Tracked coverage edits repeatedly made
   all-source receipts stale; use retained reports for status and establish one
   final source freeze before acceptance checks. Invalidations rose12 to25.
